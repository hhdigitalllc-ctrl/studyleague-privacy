How to delete your account (in the app: Profile → Delete my account, or by email)

1
Send us an email Email peakdev26@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
2
Include your username or registered email So we can locate and verify your account.
3
We'll confirm and delete within 30 days You'll receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (username, email, avatar)
  • Your rank points and exercise history
  • Your solution photos and AI grading results
  • Your subscription and free-trial status

What is retained

  • Payment records are kept for 5 years for accounting and legal compliance. These do not contain your card details.
