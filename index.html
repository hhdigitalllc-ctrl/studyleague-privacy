1. من نحن

تشرح هذه السياسة كيف نجمع بياناتك عند استخدامك تطبيق Study League، وكيف نستخدمها ونحميها.

Study League تطبيق تعليمي موجّه لطلبة البكالوريا في الجزائر، يوفّر تصحيحا آليا لتمارين البكالوريا الرسمية بواسطة الذكاء الاصطناعي، وميزات تحدّيات بين المستخدمين ولوحة ترتيب، و«المساعد الذكي» وهو مساعد دردشة بالذكاء الاصطناعي. التطبيق متاح لمن هم في سنّ الثالثة عشرة (13) فأكثر.

2. البيانات التي نجمعها

عند إنشاء حسابك

  • بريدك الإلكتروني
  • اسم المستخدم الذي تختاره
  • كلمة مرورك — مُشفّرة، لا يمكننا رؤيتها
  • صورة حسابك من Google (فقط إذا سجّلت الدخول بحساب Google — نستقبل رابط الصورة، ولا نُخزّن الصورة ذاتها)

أثناء استخدامك التطبيق

  • محاولاتك ودرجاتك وملاحظات الذكاء الاصطناعي
  • نقاط ترتيبك ومستواك
  • اشتراكك (Study Pass) وحالة تجربتك المجانية، وعمليات الشراء السابقة
  • إجاباتك في أسئلة التعارف عند البدء (الشعبة، المعدّل المستهدف، الصعوبات، ومدّة المراجعة اليومية)، نستخدمها لتخصيص تجربتك
  • مشاركتك في غرف التحدي مع مستخدمين آخرين
  • سلاسل مواظبتك، إعجاباتك، محفوظاتك
  • رمز الدعوة الذي تنشئه أو تدخله، والمكافآت المرتبطة به
  • رسائلك إلى «المساعد الذكي» وردوده: تُعالَج لحظة إرسالها للإجابة عنك (انظر القسم 4)، ولا نحتفظ بنصّ المحادثات على خوادمنا. نسجّل فقط معلومات الاستعمال: التاريخ، والجزء من التطبيق الذي طُرح منه السؤال، ونوع الباقة، وعدد وحدات المعالجة المستهلكة، وذلك لتطبيق الحدود ومراقبة التكلفة.
  • محادثاتك المحفوظة على هاتفك فقط: يحفظ التطبيق محادثاتك مع المساعد على جهازك لتعود إليها (آخر 24 رسالة في كل محادثة). تُحذف بعد 14 يوما من عدم الاستعمال، أو عند الضغط على «محادثة جديدة»، أو بحذف التطبيق، ولا تُرسَل هذه النسخة إلينا.

ما لا نحتفظ به: صور حلولك

صور حلولك المكتوبة بخطّ اليد لا تُخزَّن عندنا. نستقبلها لحظيّا فقط لتمريرها إلى محرّك التصحيح بالذكاء الاصطناعي، ثمّ تُحذف تلقائيا فور انتهاء التصحيح (سواء نجح أو فشل). نحتفظ فقط بالنتيجة (العلامة والملاحظات النصّية) لا بالصورة.

معلومات تقنية أساسية

  • نظام تشغيل جهازك وإصداره
  • إصدار التطبيق
  • معرّف الجهاز لإرسال الإشعارات (رمز Firebase Cloud Messaging)، ويُحفظ مرتبطا بحسابك
  • معرّف أندرويد (Android ID) لمنع تكرار التجربة المجانية: لا نحفظه كما هو، بل نحفظ بصمة مشفّرة أحادية الاتجاه (hash) لا يمكن إرجاعها إلى المعرّف الأصلي، ونحفظ أيضا صيغة موحّدة للبريد الإلكتروني (بأحرف صغيرة ودون الرموز بعد علامة +) لنفس الغرض. التجربة المجانية 3 أيام لكل جهاز ولكل شخص.

3. كيف نستخدم بياناتك

  • تشغيل الخدمة الأساسية: تصحيح حلولك، حساب نقاط الترتيب، إدارة اشتراكك وتجربتك المجانية.
  • تحسين جودة التصحيح: قد نراجع بعض المحاولات — بعد إزالة معلومات هويتك — لتحسين رسائل الذكاء الاصطناعي.
  • نزاهة الترتيب: نسجّل إذا خرجت من التطبيق أثناء الحلّ، وذلك لحماية عدالة لوحة الصدارة.
  • الإشعارات: نستخدم رمز جهازك لإرسال تذكيرات المذاكرة، ونتيجة التصحيح، وأخبار التمارين الجديدة. يمكنك إيقافها في أي وقت من إعدادات الإشعارات في هاتفك.
  • المساعد الذكي: نستعمل رسائلك والسياق المرتبط بها (سؤال الكويز، أو التمرين ونتيجتك، أو ملخّص إحصائياتك) للإجابة عنك فقط، ولتطبيق حدود الاستعمال ومراقبة التكلفة. لا نستعمل محادثاتك لتدريب نماذج خاصّة بنا.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your data when you use the Study League mobile app.

Study League is an educational app for Algerian Baccalauréat students. It provides AI-graded practice on official Bac exercises, plus multiplayer rooms (up to 4 players), leaderboards, and the Smart Assistant (an AI chat helper). The app is available to users aged 13 and older.

2. What data we collect

When you sign up

  • Email address
  • Chosen username
  • Password — hashed; we cannot see it
  • Google profile picture URL (only if you sign in with Google — we receive the URL, not the image itself)

While you use the app

  • Your attempts, scores, and AI feedback
  • Rank points and tier
  • Your subscription (Study Pass), free-trial status and past purchases
  • Your answers to the onboarding questions (stream, target average, challenges and daily study time), used to personalise your experience
  • Participation in multiplayer rooms with other users
  • Streak data, likes, saved exercises
  • The referral code you create or enter, and the rewards linked to it
  • Your messages to the Smart Assistant and its replies: they are processed the moment you send them in order to answer you (see section 4), and we do not keep the text of conversations on our servers. We record usage information only: the date, the part of the app the question came from, the plan type, and the number of processing units used, to apply limits and monitor cost.
  • Your conversations, saved on your phone only: the app keeps your chats with the assistant on your device so you can return to them (the last 24 messages of each chat). They are deleted after 14 days without use, when you tap "New chat", or when you uninstall the app, and this copy is never sent to us.

What we do NOT store: your solution photos

The photos of your handwritten solutions are not stored on our servers. They are held only for the split second needed to hand them to the AI grader, and are automatically deleted the moment grading finishes (whether it succeeded or failed). We keep only the result — the score and text feedback — never the image.

Basic technical information

  • Device operating system and version
  • App version
  • A device token for notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), stored linked to your account
  • Android ID, to prevent repeat free trials: we do not keep it as is. We keep a one-way cryptographic hash that cannot be reversed to the original ID, plus a normalised form of your email address (lower-case, without anything after a + sign) for the same purpose. The free trial is 3 days, once per device and per person.

3. How we use your data

  • Running the core service: grading your solutions, computing rank, managing your subscription and free trial.
  • Improving grading quality: we may review some attempts (with identifying information removed) to improve the AI's feedback.
  • Leaderboard integrity: we record whether the app was backgrounded during solving, to keep the ranking fair.
  • Notifications: we use your device token to send study reminders, grading results and news about new exercises. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's notification settings.
  • The Smart Assistant: we use your messages and the context attached to them (a quiz question, an exercise and your result, or a summary of your statistics) only to answer you, to apply usage limits and to monitor cost. We do not use your conversations to train models of our own.

4. Third parties

We rely on trusted service providers to operate. We do not sell your data.

Supabase (Supabase, Inc. — United States, servers in the EU)

All account data and photos are stored on Supabase. See their privacy policy.

Google Gemini AI (Google LLC — United States)

When you submit a solution, we send the exercise text and your solution photo(s) to Google's Gemini API to get a grade. We do not send your email or username. Governed by Google's privacy policy.

When you use the "Snap an exercise" feature, we send the photo of the exercise and/or of your answer to Google Gemini to solve or check it; the photos are deleted after processing and we keep only the text result. When you ask the Smart Assistant, we send Gemini your message and the latest messages of the conversation (up to 6), together with the context needed to answer: a quiz question and its correct answer, or the exercise text, its model answer and the grading feedback on your answer, or a summary of your statistics (your scores and success rates by unit, and your rank points). We do not send your email or username.

Chargily Pay (Algerian company)

When you buy a subscription with Edahabia or CIB, Chargily processes the payment directly. We never receive your card details.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC — United States)

To deliver notifications we use Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. The app creates a token specific to your device, which we store linked to your account and use only to send app notifications. We do not use it for advertising. We delete the token when you sign out or delete your account. Governed by Google's privacy policy.

Aptabase (Aptabase B.V. — Netherlands)

We use Aptabase to collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g. how many exercises were opened, how many gradings were run, screen navigation). No personal information is sent: not your email, not your username, not the content of your solutions. Data is governed by Aptabase's privacy policy.

TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited / TikTok Inc.)

To measure our TikTok ad campaigns, the app uses the TikTok Business SDK, which sends TikTok general events (such as app opened, account created, login, free trial started or subscription) together with your device's Advertising ID and basic technical device information. We do not send your email, your username or the content of your solutions. You can stop this measurement by deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). The data is governed by TikTok's privacy policy.

5. Data retention

  • Account data: for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after a deletion request.
  • Solution photos: automatically deleted the moment grading finishes (whether it succeeded or failed). We never retain a photo after returning its result. Uploaded photos that never get graded (e.g. because the app was force-closed) are swept automatically within a few hours.
  • Payment records: kept for 5 years for accounting compliance.
  • Device hash and normalised email (repeat-trial prevention): kept after you delete your account with no link to your identity, solely to protect against abuse.
  • Smart Assistant and grading usage log: for each request we record the date, the part of the app, the plan type and the number of processing units used (never the text of your message or your photo). What is linked to your account is deleted with it or separated from your identity.
  • Assistant conversations on your phone: they stay on your device only and are deleted as described in section 2.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct it if inaccurate
  • Delete your account and associated data (except where retention is legally required)
  • Export a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for any consent-based processing

To exercise any of these rights, email peakdev26@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data security

  • Passwords are hashed with industry standards
  • Communication with our servers is HTTPS-encrypted
  • Strict database-level isolation: no user can see another user's photos or results
  • Payments are processed by Chargily with HMAC-verified webhooks

8. Age limit and minors

Study League is available to users aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not create an account or use the app. If we discover that a user under 13 has registered, we will delete the account and their data immediately.

If you are between 13 and 18, we encourage you to make your parent or guardian aware of your use of the app, particularly regarding in-app purchases.

If you are a parent or guardian and wish to delete your child's account, email peakdev26@gmail.com and we will respond as quickly as possible.

We never use minors' data for advertising targeting, and we do not sell their data to any third party.
